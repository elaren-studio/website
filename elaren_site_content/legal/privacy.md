# Privacy Policy

We collect only the information necessary to deliver and improve our services. We do not sell your data. Contact us for any data requests.

## Service inquiries

When you send an inquiry, FormSubmit processes the details you enter and forwards them to Elaren by email. We use your name, reply email, business details, and message to assess the work and respond. Please do not include passwords, private customer details, or patient information.

The optional email-draft feature prepares your details in your browser. Opening a draft passes those details to your email application; you choose whether to send it.

## Website analytics

We use Plausible for website analytics. Marketing inquiry events record the service and source page when a visitor starts an inquiry, when FormSubmit confirms submission, or when submission cannot be confirmed. We do not include entered form details in those analytics events. A submitted inquiry is not a confirmed client engagement.

For questions about your information, email [hello@elarenstudio.com](mailto:hello@elarenstudio.com).
