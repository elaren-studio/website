The right starting point depends on where the next useful customer action is blocked. If people cannot understand or find an existing service page, begin with search and page clarity. If the offer is clear but your organization has no consistent publishing rhythm, begin with a content and social batch.

## A quick decision

Choose **local SEO** when you serve one primary business location and want to improve the connection between service-page intent, Search Console visibility, and an eligible Google Business Profile. Choose **content and social marketing** when you have approved ideas, existing assets, and up to two platforms where a planned batch can reach people who already follow or know your organization.

Neither route guarantees rankings, leads, sales, or engagement. Both begin with a written scope and a review of what you already have.

## Examples of the choice

A hypothetical Arlington home-repair company with a service page that does not explain its service area may start with local SEO page updates and a Business Profile review. A hypothetical DFW nonprofit with an upcoming workshop, a functioning website, and dormant Instagram and LinkedIn accounts may start with the content and social batch. These examples are decision aids, not promises of local demand or results.

## Three practical questions

### Do you have one location to review?

The local SEO option is scoped to one business website and primary location. If you operate online only or do not have an eligible Google Business Profile, the review centers on the website instead.

### Can someone approve source facts?

Both routes need a person who can supply accurate dates, prices, service limits, and claims to avoid. Content and social work includes one consolidated revision; local SEO proposed profile changes require owner approval before publishing.

### What happens after the first month?

Content and social continuation includes two articles or existing-page updates, another batch of twelve adapted posts, one email, and a report within the agreed scope. Local SEO continuation includes two existing-page updates, a Search Console and Business Profile check, and a report. Both are month to month with no automatic renewal; the $750 continuation rate is held for six months for the first two launch clients total across both package options.
